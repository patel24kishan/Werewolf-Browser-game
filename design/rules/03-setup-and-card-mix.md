# 03 · Setup and Card Mix

## The basic mix, for learning

New groups start with only three kinds of card: **Werewolves, one Seer and Simple
Villagers**. With this mix only the standard turn is played. The first-night setup
steps for the Thief, Cupid and the lovers are skipped.

After a game or two, add the other special roles one or two at a time.

### Small lobbies, 5 to 7 players

The digital game allows 5 to 18 players (decided 2026-11-25). Below 8, the original
rules give no mix. **Proposed** starting mix, to be tuned in playtests:

| Players | Werewolves | Seer | Simple Villagers | Suggested extra |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 5 | 1 | 1 | 3 | Swap one Villager for the Witch or the Hunter |
| 6 | 1 | 1 | 4 | Swap one Villager for the Witch or the Hunter |
| 7 | 2 | 1 | 4 | Swap one Villager for the Witch |

**Adding special roles.** Each special role (Witch, Hunter, Cupid, Little Girl, Thief)
replaces one Simple Villager. The werewolf count stays the same.

**The Thief adds cards.** With the Thief in play, deal from a deck of players + 2,
with two extra Simple Villagers added.

**Any mix is allowed.** The group can play any combination they agree on. Experienced
groups often use several special roles even with few players, because it makes the
game tenser and less predictable.

> **Tuning knob.** The werewolf count per player count is the main balance lever. The
> table above is the starting point. `/design-system` should expose it as data, not
> hard-code it.

## Setting up a game

1. **Choose a moderator.** At a table, this is a player who knows the rules well and
   does not play. In the digital version the app moderates.
2. **Deal the roles.** Shuffle the chosen cards and give one to each player, face
   down. Each player looks at their own card in secret.
3. **Thief only.** Put the two leftover cards face down in the middle.
4. **Night falls.** Everyone closes their eyes and the first night begins (see
   [04-game-flow.md](04-game-flow.md)).

## Electing the Sheriff

The Sheriff is elected by the village during the **first day**, before or after the
first discussion, whenever the moderator decides. Every player votes for one player.
The player with the most votes becomes Sheriff.

**Tied election** (decided 2026-11-25): a second round between the tied players only.
If it is still tied, one of them is picked at random. The booklet does not cover this
case.
