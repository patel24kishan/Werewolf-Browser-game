WEREWOLF

Wolves are hiding among the villagers. Each night someone dies. Each day the village votes. Who do you trust?

5–18 players15–30 minAges 10+

Chapter 1The two sides

Every player secretly gets a role. Nobody's card is shown until they are out, so anyone can lie about who they are.

The Village

Simple Villagers plus every special role. You win when every werewolf is dead and at least one villager still lives.

The Werewolves

One to four hidden killers. You win when the last villager dies and at least one wolf still lives.

Dead players still win if their side wins.

Chapter 2The roles

Start with Werewolves, one Seer and Villagers. Add the others a game or two later.

Werewolf Werewolves

Each night the wolves wake together and pick one victim, any living player, even a fellow wolf. The player with the most wolf picks dies. A tie means nobody dies. By day they pretend to be villagers.

Voting against a fellow wolf can clear your name, if the village notices.

Simple Villager Village

No power but your own judgement. Read the room, find the wolves, and don't get mistaken for one.

Seer Village

Each night, secretly learn one player's true role.

Found a wolf? Revealing yourself can expose them, but do it too early and you're next.

Witch Village

Each night you see the wolves' victim. You have one healing potion to save them and one poison to kill anyone, each usable once per game. You can use one, both, or neither in a night, even on yourself.

You get stronger late in the game. Don't waste your potions.

Hunter Village

However you are eliminated, by wolves, vote, poison or grief, you immediately shoot one other player, who dies too.

Stay hidden. Wolves who know won't give you a target.

Cupid Village

On the first night, choose two lovers, possibly yourself. If one lover dies, the other dies of grief. Lovers can never vote against each other. A wolf and a villager in love win only as the last two alive.

If you choose yourself, don't pick a loudmouth.

Little Girl Village

While the wolves hunt, you may peek to see who they are. If they catch you, you die instead of their victim.

Peek early, or you may die knowing nothing.

Thief Village

Two extra Villager cards go in the deck, and two cards are left over. On the first night you may swap your card for one of them. If both are Werewolves you must take one. You become that role.

Sheriff A title

Not dealt. The village elects a Sheriff on the first day, and the winner can't refuse. The Sheriff's vote counts twice, always on one player. When the Sheriff dies, they name a successor.

Run for it. As a wolf, don't campaign too hard.

Chapter 4How to play

Night and day alternate until one side wins. Nothing that happens at night is revealed until dawn.

First night only

  1. Thieflooks at the two leftover cards and may swap.
  2. Cupidchooses the two lovers.
  3. The loverswake, see each other, and sleep. They don't see each other's roles.

Every night

  1. Seersecretly learns one player's role.
  2. Werewolveschoose a victim. The Little Girl may peek, and dies instead if caught.
  3. Witchsees the victim and may heal them, poison anyone, both, or neither.

Every day

  1. DawnThe night's dead are revealed and fall silent. A dead Hunter shoots, a lover follows their partner, and a dead Sheriff names a successor.
  2. Sheriff electionFirst day only.
  3. DebateAccuse, defend, bluff. Anyone may claim any role.
  4. VoteEveryone points at once. The Sheriff counts double. The most votes is eliminated. A tie eliminates nobody.
  5. Night fallsBack to the Seer.

Chapter 5Winning

The Village wins when every werewolf is dead and at least one villager lives.

The Werewolves win when the last villager dies and at least one wolf lives.

Mixed lovers win, one wolf and one villager, only if they are the last two alive. While they live, neither team can win.

Chapter 6Rulings

How overlapping powers resolve. Proposed marks our rulings for cases the original rules leave open.

Deaths and chain reactions
  • A lover who dies takes their partner with them.
  • A Hunter shot that hits a lover kills both lovers.
  • A Hunter who dies of grief or poison still shoots.
  • The Sheriff names a successor when their death is revealed. Proposed
  • The game checks for a winner after each chain is fully resolved.
The night
  • The most wolf picks decides the victim. A tie or no picks kills nobody.
  • Wolves may target anyone, even another wolf.
  • A caught Little Girl dies, and the wolves' chosen victim survives.
  • The Witch can use both potions in one night, or use them on herself.
  • The Seer sees a Thief's new role, not "Thief".
The vote
  • A tie eliminates nobody.
  • The Sheriff's two votes always go to one player.
  • Lovers can't vote for each other.
  • A player who doesn't vote in time casts no vote. Proposed
  • A tied Sheriff election goes to a runoff, then a random pick.
Thief and lovers
  • A Thief who takes Cupid acts as Cupid that same night. Proposed
  • The card the Thief gives up leaves the game unseen. Proposed
  • Lovers on the same team keep their team's goal.

Chapter 7Atmosphere

The narrator makes the game. Build dread, hold back the reveal, and stir the argument when it dies down.

Night fallsThe lamps go out across the village. Close your eyes… and pray the night is quiet.
Werewolves wakeWerewolves, wake. Find your pack… and choose your prey.
DawnThe sun rises. Everyone wakes… except one.
The voteEnough talk. On my word, point to the one you believe is the beast.
